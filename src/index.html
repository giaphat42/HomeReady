EVIDENCE BEFORE CONFIDENCE

Does the model beat a simple average?

Six rolling one-month validation forecasts. Each prediction uses earlier months only. Lower mean absolute error (MAE) is better. The best method is selected separately for income and expenses.

Series Ridge MAE 3-month mean MAE Selected method

How it learns and what it cannot tell you

Ridge regression learns coefficients for a time trend, annual sine/cosine seasonality and a December indicator. Features are standardised using training data only; regularisation is fixed at alpha = 1. After validation, the model is fitted to all 36 months. If the three-month average has lower or equal error, it is used instead.

The earlier 33 months are invented training fixtures. The final three match your selected sample-account snapshot. This demonstrates the pipeline, not accuracy on real customers. Validation scores also choose the method, so they are not an independent test or evidence of six-month accuracy.

The stress range uses twice the sum of selected one-step MAEs, with a 5% cash-flow floor, scaled by the square root of the horizon. It is a heuristic, not a 95% confidence interval. Job loss, unusual bills, policy changes and structural breaks can fall outside it.

No savings interest, investment returns or house-price growth is modelled. The existing home-plan timeline remains a constant-contribution scenario. This forecast does not silently replace it or extrapolate years into the future.

PROTOTYPE PRIVACY NOTICE · 17 NOVEMBER 2026

A clear view of your data.

This HomeReady hackathon prototype is for exploring fictional savings and mortgage scenarios. It is not a bank and does not provide real account aggregation.

Information and purpose

The app uses the planning values you enter, selected fictional account data, and privacy choices to calculate and explain your home plan, including a locally trained cash-flow forecast in Automatic mode. Choosing manual mode avoids sample-account processing. Please do not enter bank credentials or real personal financial records.

Storage and retention

Session-only mode uses memory and discards app changes when the page reloads or closes. “Remember on this device” saves the workspace to this browser’s local storage until you turn it off or clear data. Chat messages, learned forecasting coefficients and the forecasting expense scenario remain in memory and are not stored by the app.

Disclosure and external services

The app has no bank API, external AI service, analytics, advertising pixels or external font requests. The forecasting model trains locally in your browser on synthetic monthly history. Its code does not transmit form inputs or chats to a server. The hosting platform separately manages authentication, delivery and access logs under its own policies. External reference links open other providers’ websites.

Your choices

You can disconnect samples, withdraw sample-data permission, change storage choices, export your plan or clear app data in Privacy & control. Clearing this app does not delete hosting-platform records or exported files.

Scope and contact

This is a notice for the current prototype, not a production legal agreement or compliance certification. For this demo, contact the project owner through the channel that supplied your demo link or source. A real service requires a named legal operator, contact and complaint channels, retention rules, security controls and review of its actual providers before collecting customer data.
